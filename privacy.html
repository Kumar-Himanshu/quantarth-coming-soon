Last updated: October 2026 — Subject to owner review before publication

1. Introduction

QuantArth (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the website at https://quantarth.com and provides developer APIs and related technology services. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with our website and services.

By using our website, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our website or services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact information: When you send us an email, we receive your email address and the content of your message.
  • Usage data: Standard server logs may record your IP address, browser type, referring URL, and pages visited. We do not use third-party analytics scripts on this website.
  • API usage data: If you access our developer APIs, we may log request metadata such as timestamps, endpoint paths, and response status codes for operational purposes.

We do not collect payment information, government-issued identification, or sensitive personal data through this website.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to enquiries sent to admin@quantarth.com
  • Operate, maintain, and improve our website and API services
  • Monitor for security issues and troubleshoot technical problems

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Cookies and Tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking scripts. Our web infrastructure (Cloudflare) may set technical cookies necessary for security and performance. These are not used to track you across other websites.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website is served through Cloudflare. Cloudflare processes network traffic as part of its service. Please refer to Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy for details on how they handle data.

Google Fonts are loaded from Google’s servers when you visit this website. Google may log your IP address as part of this service.

6. Data Retention

We retain email enquiries for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to and manage the enquiry. Specific retention periods for operational logs are determined by our infrastructure configuration and are subject to review.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, or request deletion of data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at admin@quantarth.com.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information transmitted to and from our website. However, no internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of information sent to us.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will indicate the date of the last update at the top of this page. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at admin@quantarth.com.
